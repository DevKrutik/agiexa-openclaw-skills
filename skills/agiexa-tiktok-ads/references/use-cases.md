# TikTok Ads use cases via Agiexa

Example prompts a merchant will be able to give their agent after Agiexa launches (November 2026). They are illustrations of future use - do not run them today.

More examples and live demos: https://agiexa.com/integrations/tiktok-ads

## Identify Your Most Profitable Products

Compare TikTok ad performance with Shopify sales to find products worth testing or scaling.

- Apps: TikTok Ads, Shopify
- Tools: `TIKTOK_ADS_LIST_CAMPAIGNS`, `TIKTOK_ADS_GET_AD_BENCHMARK_REPORT`, `TIKTOK_ADS_GET_SMART_PLUS_MATERIAL_REPORT`

> Analyze my Shopify product sales alongside TikTok Ads campaign performance for the last 30 days. Rank the products with the strongest combination of Shopify revenue, attributed ad revenue, and advertising efficiency. Identify products spending money without generating enough attributed sales. If product costs, refunds, and margins are available, include them in the analysis. Clearly distinguish observed metrics from estimates and recommend which products deserve further testing.

## Discover Trending Products and Keywords

Use TikTok trend insights to find catalog opportunities before creating new campaigns.

- Apps: TikTok Ads, Shopify
- Tools: `TIKTOK_ADS_LIST_TRENDING_SEARCH_KEYWORDS`, `TIKTOK_ADS_LIST_TRENDING_CATALOG_PRODUCTS`, `TIKTOK_ADS_GET_CATALOG_CATEGORY_INSIGHTS`, `TIKTOK_ADS_LIST_RECOMMENDED_SEARCH_KEYWORDS`

> Review trending TikTok search keywords, trending catalog products, and relevant category insights available to my account. Compare these trends with my Shopify product catalog and recent sales. Identify the five most relevant product opportunities, explain why each may appeal to TikTok users, and suggest a video concept and search keyword for each. Do not claim that a trend guarantees sales, and identify any product availability or data gaps.

## Find Ads With Creative Fatigue

Detect weakening creatives and connect them to the relevant Shopify products.

- Apps: TikTok Ads, Shopify
- Tools: `TIKTOK_ADS_LIST_CREATIVE_FATIGUE_RESULTS`, `TIKTOK_ADS_GET_VIDEO_PERFORMANCE_REPORT`, `TIKTOK_ADS_LIST_ADS`

> Review my TikTok ad performance, video metrics, and available creative-fatigue results. Match each ad to its Shopify product where the relationship can be established. Identify creatives that appear to be losing effectiveness, explain the evidence, and propose three alternative video concepts for each affected product. Consider Shopify sales alongside advertising metrics, and do not disable or replace any existing ad without my approval.

## Prepare Smart+ Campaign Launches

Build a proposed campaign around a Shopify product, with review before anything activates.

- Apps: TikTok Ads, Shopify
- Tools: `TIKTOK_ADS_CREATE_SMART_PLUS_CAMPAIGN`, `TIKTOK_ADS_CREATE_SMART_PLUS_ADGROUP`, `TIKTOK_ADS_CREATE_SMART_PLUS_AD`, `TIKTOK_ADS_SMART_PLUS_AD_PREVIEW`

> Analyze my Shopify products and recent TikTok Ads performance. Recommend one product for a new Smart+ campaign, explaining the selection using available sales, advertising, and product information. Prepare a proposed campaign structure with suggested ad groups, creative concepts, audience settings where supported, schedule, and budget. Show the complete configuration for my review before creating anything. Keep new campaign resources disabled for review where the platform's creation flow requires it, and never activate advertising or spend money without my explicit approval.

## Optimize Existing Campaign Budgets

Find budget allocation opportunities using ad performance and Shopify context, approval first.

- Apps: TikTok Ads, Shopify
- Tools: `TIKTOK_ADS_LIST_AD_GROUPS`, `TIKTOK_ADS_UPDATE_SMART_PLUS_ADGROUP_BUDGETS`, `TIKTOK_ADS_LIST_ADVERTISER_BUDGET_CHANGES`, `TIKTOK_ADS_GET_BID_PROTECTION_DETAILS`

> Review my TikTok campaign and ad-group performance against Shopify sales for the last 14 days. Identify campaigns that may deserve more budget, less budget, or further testing. Consider attributed revenue, spend, conversion performance, product margins if available, inventory, and recent changes. Prepare a proposed budget allocation with the expected rationale and risks. Do not update any account, campaign, or ad-group budget until I approve the exact changes.

## Investigate Weak Product Conversions

Find ads that attract engagement but don't convert into Shopify orders.

- Apps: TikTok Ads, Shopify
- Tools: `TIKTOK_ADS_GET_VIDEO_PERFORMANCE_REPORT`, `TIKTOK_ADS_LIST_ADS`, `TIKTOK_ADS_GET_AD_BENCHMARK_REPORT`

> Find TikTok ads and videos that receive meaningful engagement or traffic but show weak conversion performance in the available reporting. Match them to Shopify products and compare the results with store sales, product pricing, availability, and product-page information where accessible. Separate confirmed data from hypotheses. Recommend changes to the video hook, product demonstration, offer presentation, or landing experience. Do not assume that TikTok metrics and Shopify orders share identical attribution windows.

## Monitor Advertising Spend and Balances

Review balances, billing activity, and budget changes to prevent unexpected spend.

- Apps: TikTok Ads, Shopify
- Tools: `TIKTOK_ADS_LIST_ADVERTISER_BALANCES`, `TIKTOK_ADS_LIST_ADVERTISER_TRANSACTIONS`, `TIKTOK_ADS_LIST_ADVERTISER_BUDGET_CHANGES`, `TIKTOK_ADS_GET_BUSINESS_CENTER_BALANCE`

> Review the available TikTok advertiser account balances, campaign budgets, recent budget-change records, and billing transactions. Summarize current spending capacity, notable changes, and any unusual entries that need investigation. Compare this with Shopify sales over the same period, while keeping advertising spend separate from Shopify revenue. Flag campaigns that may need attention and recommend a spending plan. Do not change budgets or move funds without my explicit approval.

## Improve Product Video Performance

Use in-second video performance and fatigue data to build better creative testing plans.

- Apps: TikTok Ads, Shopify
- Tools: `TIKTOK_ADS_GET_VIDEO_PERFORMANCE_REPORT`, `TIKTOK_ADS_LIST_CREATIVE_FATIGUE_RESULTS`, `TIKTOK_ADS_GET_CREATIVE_ASSETS`

> Review the available in-second TikTok video performance data and creative-fatigue results for ads promoting my Shopify products. Identify which videos hold attention, where viewers appear to drop off, and which creative concepts deserve further testing. For each product, suggest three alternative video concepts with a hook, demonstration, key benefit, and call to action. Use actual available metrics when possible, label assumptions clearly, and do not claim that a proposed creative will guarantee better results.

## Coordinate Marketing and Inventory

Avoid promoting products that can't meet demand by combining Shopify inventory with ad data.

- Apps: TikTok Ads, Shopify
- Tools: `TIKTOK_ADS_LIST_CAMPAIGNS`, `TIKTOK_ADS_LIST_SHOWCASE_PRODUCTS`, `TIKTOK_ADS_GET_AD_BENCHMARK_REPORT`

> Review my Shopify product availability and recent sales alongside TikTok campaign performance. Identify products that are selling well through advertising but may have limited stock, and products with sufficient availability that could be candidates for further promotion. Where Shopify inventory data is accessible, include available quantities and relevant product variants. Recommend whether to maintain, test, or scale promotion for each product. Do not change campaign budgets or inventory records without approval.

## Create a Daily Advertising Briefing

A daily view of TikTok performance, Shopify sales, risks, and the top three actions.

- Apps: TikTok Ads, Shopify, Slack
- Tools: `TIKTOK_ADS_LIST_CAMPAIGNS`, `TIKTOK_ADS_GET_VIDEO_PERFORMANCE_REPORT`, `TIKTOK_ADS_LIST_TRENDING_SEARCH_KEYWORDS`, `TIKTOK_ADS_LIST_ADVERTISER_BALANCES`

> Prepare a daily marketing briefing using the latest available TikTok Ads reports and Shopify sales data. Include advertising spend, attributed revenue, campaign efficiency, leading products, underperforming creatives, relevant search trends, account balance concerns, and product availability risks where the data is accessible. Compare results with the previous period when possible. Highlight the three most important actions for today, explain the evidence behind each recommendation, and identify any data or attribution limitations. Do not make changes to campaigns or budgets automatically.
