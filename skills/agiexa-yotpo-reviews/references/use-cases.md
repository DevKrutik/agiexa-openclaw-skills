# Yotpo Reviews use cases via Agiexa

Example prompts a merchant will be able to give their agent after Agiexa launches (November 2026). They are illustrations of future use - do not run them today.

More examples and live demos: https://agiexa.com/integrations/yotpo-reviews

## Find Your Highest-Rated Products

Rank Shopify products by Yotpo ratings and review counts to find customer favorites.

- Apps: Yotpo Reviews, Shopify
- Tools: `YOTPO_PRODUCT_RATING_SUMMARY`, `YOTPO_GET_PRODUCT_RATING`

> Analyze Yotpo ratings and reviews for products in my Shopify store. Rank products by average rating and review count, and identify products with consistently strong customer feedback. Highlight products with high ratings supported by a meaningful number of reviews. If Shopify sales data is available, compare ratings with sales performance to identify products worth featuring in marketing campaigns. Clearly distinguish rating-based findings from sales-based findings.

## Detect Recurring Product Complaints

Group repeated concerns in reviews and prioritize products needing improvement.

- Apps: Yotpo Reviews, Shopify
- Tools: `YOTPO_LIST_REVIEWS`, `YOTPO_GET_REVIEW`, `YOTPO_GET_PRODUCT_RATING`

> Review recent Yotpo reviews for my Shopify products, prioritizing low-rated reviews and recurring complaints. Group the feedback into themes such as quality, durability, sizing, usability, packaging, and delivery where relevant. Count reviews mentioning each theme when possible and identify the affected products. Recommend practical follow-up actions and distinguish verified customer statements from your interpretation. Do not assume a complaint proves a product defect without further investigation.

## Improve Shopify Product Pages

Use feedback and unanswered questions to find missing product information.

- Apps: Yotpo Reviews, Shopify
- Tools: `YOTPO_LIST_QUESTIONS`, `YOTPO_LIST_REVIEWS`

> Analyze Yotpo product reviews and customer questions for my Shopify catalog. Identify recurring questions, confusing product details, and unmet customer expectations. Match the feedback to the relevant Shopify products where possible. Recommend specific improvements to product descriptions, specifications, FAQs, images, or usage instructions. Use existing review answers and verified product information only; do not invent product specifications. Present the proposed changes for review rather than editing product pages automatically.

## Draft Helpful Review Responses

Prepare empathetic, accurate responses to negative reviews for your approval.

- Apps: Yotpo Reviews, Shopify
- Tools: `YOTPO_LIST_REVIEWS`, `YOTPO_GET_REVIEW`, `YOTPO_COMMENT_ON_REVIEW`

> Review recent negative Yotpo reviews for my Shopify products and draft a helpful response for each relevant review. Acknowledge the customer's concern, use an appropriate tone, and suggest a practical next step. Avoid arguing with customers, disclosing private information, or making unsupported refund or replacement promises. Identify whether each proposed response should be public or private. Show me the exact review and proposed response for approval before posting any comment.

## Identify Products Needing Attention

Combine review trends with Shopify sales to prioritize experience improvements.

- Apps: Yotpo Reviews, Shopify
- Tools: `YOTPO_PRODUCT_RATING_SUMMARY`, `YOTPO_GET_PRODUCT_RATING`, `YOTPO_LIST_REVIEWS`

> Compare Yotpo product ratings and review trends with Shopify product sales where available. Identify products with declining ratings, recurring complaints, or high sales combined with weak customer feedback. Rank the products that need attention and explain the evidence behind each recommendation. Include review counts and dates where available, and account for small sample sizes. Recommend investigation or product-page improvements without automatically changing listings or hiding reviews.

## Discover Customer Purchase Motivations

Extract recurring positive feedback that reveals why customers value products.

- Apps: Yotpo Reviews, Shopify
- Tools: `YOTPO_LIST_REVIEWS`, `YOTPO_PRODUCT_RATING_SUMMARY`

> Analyze positive Yotpo reviews for my Shopify products. Identify recurring benefits, features, use cases, and customer motivations mentioned by reviewers. Group the insights by product and summarize the strongest recurring themes with representative paraphrases. Suggest product-page messaging and advertising angles based on genuine customer feedback. Do not fabricate testimonials, alter the meaning of reviews, or present paraphrased feedback as a direct customer quote.

## Compare Product Ratings Over Time

Investigate rating changes using review dates and product-level summaries.

- Apps: Yotpo Reviews, Shopify
- Tools: `YOTPO_LIST_REVIEWS`, `YOTPO_PRODUCT_RATING_SUMMARY`

> Analyze Yotpo reviews and product ratings for my Shopify store over the latest available period. Compare recent feedback with an earlier period when the necessary historical data is available. Identify products with improving or declining sentiment, changes in review volume, and emerging complaint themes. Explain whether the evidence is sufficient to support each conclusion. If historical snapshots or complete review history are unavailable, state that limitation instead of inventing a trend.

## Find Products With Weak Social Proof

Spot products with few reviews or low ratings that need better feedback visibility.

- Apps: Yotpo Reviews, Shopify
- Tools: `YOTPO_PRODUCT_RATING_SUMMARY`, `YOTPO_GET_PRODUCT_RATING`, `YOTPO_SET_REVIEW_VISIBILITY`

> Review Yotpo product ratings and review counts across my Shopify catalog. Identify products with few reviews, missing ratings, or comparatively weak customer feedback. Compare these findings with product sales where available. Recommend ethical ways to improve review collection from eligible customers and improve the visibility of genuine reviews. Do not generate fake reviews, manipulate ratings, or hide legitimate negative feedback merely to improve product appearance.

## Turn Questions Into Sales Insights

Analyze customer questions to surface buying objections and FAQ gaps.

- Apps: Yotpo Reviews, Shopify
- Tools: `YOTPO_LIST_QUESTIONS`, `YOTPO_LIST_REVIEWS`

> Analyze customer questions and available answers in Yotpo for my Shopify products. Group recurring questions by product and topic, identify questions that appear unanswered, and highlight potential buying objections. Match the questions to Shopify product information where available. Recommend clear answers and product-page FAQ improvements using verified information. Do not assume a question caused a lost sale unless reliable conversion data supports that conclusion.

## Build a Weekly Review Briefing

Ratings, complaints, and positive themes rolled into a weekly action plan.

- Apps: Yotpo Reviews, Shopify, Slack
- Tools: `YOTPO_PRODUCT_RATING_SUMMARY`, `YOTPO_LIST_REVIEWS`, `YOTPO_LIST_QUESTIONS`

> Prepare a weekly customer feedback briefing for my Shopify store using available Yotpo reviews, product ratings, and customer questions. Include the highest-rated products, products with the weakest ratings, recurring positive themes, major complaints, unanswered questions, and changes over time where historical data exists. Compare findings with Shopify sales if available. Recommend the three most important actions for product improvement and customer experience. Clearly state data limitations and do not automatically publish responses or change review visibility.
