[Agiexa](https://agiexa.com) is a connection layer between AI agents, Shopify, and the other apps a merchant runs their store with. Merchants approve each agent device and choose exactly which tools it can use. Risky actions ask for approval first, and every action is logged.

These OpenClaw skills are a **preview**. Agiexa launches in **November 2026**. Until then, the skills explain what your agent will be able to do (tools, triggers and real use cases) and can add you to the launch waitlist if you ask.

## What you can ask your agent

- "What will Agiexa let you do in my Shopify store?"
- "Show me how you'd handle refund emails with Gmail and Shopify."
- "Which Klaviyo tools will you have access to?"
- "Add me to the Agiexa waitlist."

## Your permission comes first

- The skills **cannot access** your store or any app today, and they never claim to.
- Your agent only adds you to the waitlist **if you ask or agree**. It asks for your details, shows you exactly what will be sent, and waits for you to confirm.
- Only what you provide (email, role, an optional link and message) is sent to agiexa.com. Nothing about your store, files or conversations is sent.
- The details are used only to keep you updated about Agiexa's launch. Email [hello@agiexa.com](mailto:hello@agiexa.com) to be removed.

You can also join directly at [agiexa.com](https://agiexa.com) using the "I'm Interested" button.
